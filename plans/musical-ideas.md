# Musical notification ideas

Ideas for later exploration; these are not part of the current implementation.

- **Factory voices:** Give machine categories different instruments or pitch
  ranges, so players can hear which parts of the factory are improving.
- **Milestone flourishes:** Play a short celebration for a first upgrade to a new
  quality or a machine reaching maximum quality.
- **Signatures per machine:** Give each machine a stable base note or motif that
  becomes familiar and could rise as its quality improves.
- **Quality pitch:** Spread quality ranks across the available notes or octaves,
  including modded games with hundreds of quality levels. Keep the mapping stable
  as research progresses; nearby qualities can share a pitch.
- **Melodies:** Advance through a short looping tune with each audible upgrade.
- **Call and response:** Let machine and module upgrades play answering phrases.
- **Percussion patterns:** Give drum sounds their own sequences or alternating hits.
- **Timed bursts:** Group nearby upgrades into a paced run, with a strict backlog
  limit. Unlike the current staircase, this would need timing and scheduling.
- **Preview:** Audition a single notification and a busy burst from the settings UI.
